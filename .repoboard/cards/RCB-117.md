## Evidence

`packages/server/test/repo-watch.test.ts` K12 T4 writes `fresh.txt`, sleeps a fixed 2500 ms,
then expects `scanCount()` to have risen and `fresh.txt` in the scan. The rescan debounce is
2000 ms (`http.ts` `rescanDebounceMs ?? 2000`), so the margin is 500 ms for chokidar's event,
the debounce timer and the scan itself. Logged failures in full gates: 09-22 ×3 (33297da gate,
1271|4 gate, 1318|4 gate), 09-23 ×2 (RCB-113 leg 1 baseline, leg 2 run 1). Each time it passed
alone or on rerun. No logged failure message says which of the two expects failed — not measured.
