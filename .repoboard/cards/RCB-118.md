## Why

The coordinator's standing ask (09-22), agreed 09-23 04:3xZ. On an empty board `seat <name>` prints
`(no todo card)` and nothing else, so a cold seat cannot tell "nothing to do" apart from "work
exists but it is gated, owner-held, or someone else's". Second, folded in on the coordinator's
word: `.repoboard/STATE.md` on disk always holds `_(generated from open decisions)_` under OWNER
QUEUE. A raw-file reader takes that for an empty queue (the coordinator did, 09-23 04:26Z).

## DoD

- No next card → one line of counts: todo assigned to other seats, non-done cards blocked on a
  gate, cards waiting on an owner decision. Zeros print as 0, not omitted. The same counts go in
  the bundle for `--json`.
- The on-disk placeholder says it is not stored there and names `repoboard state`. The display
  rendering with no open decisions keeps its current text (it IS `repoboard state`).
- Tests for both. A seat-run control per guarantee. Gate ×2; typecheck/lint/build 0.
