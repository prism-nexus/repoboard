## Evidence

RCB-148's measurement (its card note has the full table): with N cards in one column, a sort
toggle takes 77-135 ms at N=300 and 124-201 ms at N=500; clearing a size filter (remounting the
hidden cards) takes 166-200 ms at 300 and 194-318 ms at 500. The budget for a click response is
100 ms. Opening the drawer takes 48-78 ms at both sizes, so a selection likely re-renders every card.

## Do

1. Profile one sort toggle at N=500 in a VISIBLE tab (React profiler plus the Performance panel)
   and record where the time goes before changing anything.
2. `React.memo` on `CardItem`, with stable callbacks; re-measure the same four interactions.
3. Only if sort or remount is still over 100 ms at N=500: virtualize the column, keeping dnd-kit
   drag working (a drop onto an off-screen card) — that design goes in a brief first.

The generator for the boards and the timing script are in the seat's log block for 2026-09-24.
