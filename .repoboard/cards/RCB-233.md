## Problem

Seats on a member board type a clock time into every `repoboard log` title by hand (`… 8:07 pm PST 11-01 (04:07Z
11-02): …`), although the heading already carries the tool's ISO stamp. Over five weeks, 135 of that board's 189 log
corrections fixed a wrong typed time, and it is the one correction type that grows with session length.

## Want

`board.yml` `timezone:` (IANA). With it, `repoboard log` (CLI and `append_repo_log`) starts the title with the local
time (`8:07 pm PST 11-01 · <title>`), so a seat never types one. With or without it, a title whose first `HH:MMZ` (or
`HH:MxZ` window) is more than 5 min from the block's stamp gets one warning line (exit 0, block written). Without
`timezone:` the heading is byte-identical to today. Brief: local layer `wip/RCB-233-BRIEF.md`.
